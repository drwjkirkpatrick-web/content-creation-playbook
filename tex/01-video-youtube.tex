\section*{The First 30 Seconds}
\begin{itemize}[label=\tlabel]
\tip{1. Hook within 5-10 seconds}{State the exact outcome the viewer gets; a promise confirmation, pattern interrupt, or immediate payoff.}
\tip{2. Match the hook to the title exactly}{The viewer needs immediate visual and spoken confirmation they're in the right place.}
\tip{3. Show visual proof in under 10 seconds}{Display the finished result before explaining how you got it.}
\tip{4. Cut all preamble}{No logo stings, channel animations, or throat-clearing - be into step one before the 25-second mark.}
\tip{5. Open a curiosity loop before step one}{Plant a seed about the mistake or shortcut coming later, then pay it off.}
\end{itemize}
\section*{Holding Attention}
\begin{itemize}[label=\tlabel]
\tip{6. Front-load value by the 25-40\% mark}{Deliver the core answer early; depth and nuance come after the payoff, not before it.}
\tip{7. Interrupt patterns every 30-60 seconds}{B-roll, graphics, angle changes, and cuts re-engage drifting attention.}
\tip{8. Re-hook every 60-90 seconds}{Micro-teasers and open loops at section boundaries stop silent bleed.}
\tip{9. Distribute micro-payoffs}{Small wins of value throughout the video, not one payoff at the end.}
\tip{10. Make the viewer the protagonist}{Use 'you' over 'I'; every section should be a step toward the question the video answers.}
\end{itemize}
\section*{Structure and Iteration}
\begin{itemize}[label=\tlabel]
\tip{11. Add timestamped chapters}{Navigability is retention; let viewers self-select without leaving.}
\tip{12. Film two hooks and compare}{Publish the stronger opening, check retention at 48 hours, and reuse the loser later.}
\tip{13. End with one specific next action}{A single end-screen target beats a menu of options.}
\end{itemize}
