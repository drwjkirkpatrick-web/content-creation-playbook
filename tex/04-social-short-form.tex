\section*{The Hook}
\begin{itemize}[label=\tlabel]
\tip{1. Hook in the first 2-3 seconds}{Bold visual, surprising object, or direct address - the swipe decision happens immediately.}
\tip{2. Write 20 hooks before filming}{Spend ten minutes on hook variants for one topic; treat hooks as data, not taste.}
\tip{3. Design the first frame visually}{The frame must work with sound off: bold text, expressive face, or unusual context.}
\tip{4. Script three beats: hook, proof, payoff}{Hook 0-3s, proof 3-30s, payoff/CTA in the last 5s.}
\end{itemize}
\section*{Format and Platform}
\begin{itemize}[label=\tlabel]
\tip{5. Optimize completion, not duration}{A 45-second video with 70\% completion beats a 15-second with 40\%; cut to the payoff, not a target length.}
\tip{6. Use 1-3 hashtags, mixed broad and niche}{One trending, one community, one descriptive - the algorithm punishes obvious bait.}
\tip{7. Say your keywords out loud}{Speech recognition feeds content classification; captions should echo search phrases.}
\tip{8. Remove watermarks when repurposing}{Cross-post the same 9:16 file, but strip TikTok marks before Reels or Shorts.}
\tip{9. Pick formats, not trends}{Challenges, comedy, reactions, and POV explainers keep earning; pick the format before the topic.}
\end{itemize}
\section*{Cadence and Iteration}
\begin{itemize}[label=\tlabel]
\tip{10. Post 3-7 times per week}{Daily cadence for the first 90 days builds the niche signal; consistency beats weekly excellence.}
\tip{11. Batch film 5-10 videos per session}{Set up once, record while in position, schedule across the week.}
\tip{12. Track saves and kills your bottom third}{Saves are 2-3x more valuable than likes; review first-3-second retention per hook monthly.}
\end{itemize}
